# Veloracia terms of use

**Last updated: 27 September 2026**

These terms describe the intended use of Veloracia, a location-based voice radio for web and mobile. The service lets guests listen to a shared feed, join a speaking queue, and send short voice messages to people nearby.

## Using the service

- Use a nickname and avatar that do not impersonate or harass another person.
- Use the talk feature lawfully and respectfully. Do not send threats, hate speech, explicit abuse, spam, or content that violates another person's rights.
- Do not attempt to disrupt the service, access another guest's account, or bypass its usage limits.
- Location sharing is optional. If you grant location access and join the queue or send a voice message, your coordinates may be shared with other signed-in guests.

The app applies a client-side limit of 10 voice messages per hour and a 30-second recording limit. Client-side limits can be bypassed and are not a guarantee against abuse.

## Your content and data

You remain responsible for the voice messages you send. Sending a message allows the service to store and make it available to signed-in guests as needed to operate the radio. The current app does not provide a control to delete a sent message. See the [privacy notice](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for storage and retention details.

## Availability

The service depends on third-party hosting, Firebase, device permissions, and network access. Features may be unavailable or change over time. The service is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted access, accurate location, or permanent data storage.

## Operator details

The project repository does not identify the service operator, legal jurisdiction, or a support contact. Those details and any terms required for the intended launch regions must be added by the operator before public release.
